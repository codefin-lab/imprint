# Why this project

Onboarding is the slowest step in the customer journey, and the easiest to fix.

## Onboarding takes five days today

- A new customer waits five working days on average
- Three systems hold customer data, and none of them is complete
- Staff re-key the same details at every step
- One in four applications is returned for missing documents

<!-- notes: Open with the number the client already knows. Five days is their own figure. -->

## One platform replaces three hand-offs

Customers apply once, in the app or at a branch. Every team works from the same record, and the core system is updated automatically.
