# Minutes of meeting and technical reports

## Minutes of meeting

Minutes record what was **decided** and who will **do** what by when. They are not a
transcript. Template: `templates/mom.md`.

- **Header**: meeting title, date, time, place or channel, attendees from each side,
  absentees, who took the minutes.
- **Decisions**: numbered, one decision per row, with who agreed. Write what was agreed,
  not what was discussed.
- **Action items**: a table of `#`, action, owner, due date. Every action has one named owner
  and a date; "the team" and "soon" are not owners or dates.
- **Open points**: anything left undecided, stated as open rather than implied as agreed.
- **Next meeting**: date and purpose.
- Send within one working day, and ask for corrections by a stated date; silence after that
  is taken as agreement.

## Technical report

A report answers a question the reader asked: an assessment, a sizing, an incident review, a
recommendation. Template: `templates/report.md`.

- **Start with the answer**: a summary of findings and recommendations on the first page.
- **Then the evidence**: method, data, analysis, each finding traceable to its evidence.
- **Recommendations are actionable**: what to do, who, the priority, and the expected effect.
- **Assumptions and limits** stated, so the reader knows how far the conclusions reach.
- For an incident review: timeline in UTC or one stated time zone, impact, root cause,
  contributing factors, and actions with owners, written without blame.
